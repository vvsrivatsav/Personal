The call I’m proudest of

The program was set up to archive region by region, each on its own local instance — which meant standing up a separate managed file transfer setup every single time a new region came online.

I drove the shift to a single global instance with multiple Unity Catalogs. Same compliance posture, one less bottleneck per region, and a roadmap that stops paying the same tax twice.

Swiggy Seamless Support Handoff

Sep 2026

Product case study — research, PRD and live prototype

When automated support can't resolve a post-order issue, the transfer to a human succeeds technically and fails experientially: the conversation moves, the case doesn't. The customer starts over.

I proposed an editable AI-assisted handoff brief that carries the order, issue, evidence, prior bot action and requested outcome into the agent's first reply — reviewed by the customer before it is sent. The AI summarises and flags missing context; refund eligibility, amount and fraud treatment stay with existing policy systems. Scoped to wrong, missing and damaged-item cases, with first-handoff resolution rate as the primary metric.

8 of 10 escalated interviewees repeated information they had already given. Only 3 of 10 accepted the outcome they got.

WhatsApp Business Promotion Request Gate

Aug 2026

Product discovery and prototype

I ran 10 user interviews and collected 41 survey responses to test a hunch about unwanted business messages. Unapproved promotions clutter personal conversations and quietly erode people's sense of control over their own inbox.

The answer was a request gate: promotions route to a review inbox where you can allow, reject, or report the business, while OTPs, bills, and transaction messages stay where you need them. I wrote the full PRD — flow, stories, acceptance criteria, edge cases, metrics, open questions — and published an interactive prototype with a walkthrough video.

37 of 41 survey respondents had received promotions they never asked for. The same problem surfaced in 8 of the 10 interviews.
